COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 3 working days
before the requested leave date.
Annual leave requires approval from the employee's manager.

Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate is required when sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.

Work From Home

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
Work-from-home requests must be approved by the employee's manager.
Employees working from home must remain available during normal working hours.

Employee Benefits

Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance.
Health insurance coverage begins after 30 days of employment.
Employees can add eligible dependents to their health insurance plan.

Working Hours

Normal working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.
